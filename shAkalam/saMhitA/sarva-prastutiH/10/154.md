154 (980)  
To a Dead Man  
Yamī  
5 verses: anuṣṭubh  
This hymn has a parallel in the Atharvaveda (XVIIII.2.14–18), where it is found  among the funeral verses, and in the ritual sūtras it is used at the burning of the  corpse.
