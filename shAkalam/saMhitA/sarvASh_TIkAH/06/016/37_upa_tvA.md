“Strength-begotten **Agni**, we, offering (sacrificial) food, address praises to you who are of plural asing aspect.”

</details>

     O Agni, have poured forth our songs.

</details>

<details><summary>दयानन्द-सरस्वती (हि) - पदार्थः</summary>

</details>
